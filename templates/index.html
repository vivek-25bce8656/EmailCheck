Methodology, RFC Compliance & Transparency

Why EmailCheck provides evidence-based assessments rather than absolute mailbox delivery claims:

  • DNS and MX records prove mail infrastructure, not individual inbox existence: Having valid MX records proves the domain is configured to accept mail via designated mail servers. It cannot confirm whether a specific user mailbox exists without sending mail or intrusive probing.
  • RFC 5321 Section 5.1 Fallback Rules: If a domain lacks explicit MX records, email protocol specifications dictate falling back to the domain's A or AAAA address. EmailCheck automatically inspects this fallback before declaring a domain non-routable.
  • No Intrusive SMTP Probing: Cloud environments (including Render, AWS, and GCP) block outbound port 25 to prevent spam. Furthermore, modern mail providers (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365) employ greylisting, catch-all configurations, and anti-harvesting defenses that render VRFY/RCPT probing inaccurate or counterproductive.
  • Formula Injection Shielding: When exporting assessment data to CSV, all fields are sanitized against formula execution (e.g. commands starting with =, +, -, or @), ensuring spreadsheet safety.
